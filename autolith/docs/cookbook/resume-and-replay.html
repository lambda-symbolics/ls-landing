Find and resume past work

Conversations are saved as they happen. Yesterday's debugging session is one command away, and read-only replay lets you look without continuing.

Resume a conversation

$ autolith resume

Pick a saved conversation from the list and continue where you left off. Inside a session, /resume switches without leaving Autolith, and /new starts fresh while keeping the current conversation saved.

Find the right session

/conversations lists saved conversations with their titles and timestamps. Providers can generate session titles for you; /titles turns that on or off.

Look without continuing

$ autolith replay

Replay opens a saved conversation read-only, so you can check what happened without resuming it. When a resumed conversation needs more of its past in view, /history loads earlier transcript turns into the session.
